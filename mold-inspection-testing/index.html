Mold Inspection & Testing in Saratoga Springs

A mold inspection is a visual and moisture assessment of your property; testing (air or surface sampling) can help confirm what is present and where. Most people call after seeing discoloration, smelling mustiness, or finding water damage.

Signs you may need an inspection

  • Musty odor in a basement, closet or crawl space
  • Staining on ceilings or walls after a leak
  • Condensation on windows or peeling paint
  • Health symptoms that ease when you leave the property

What the process usually involves

A provider checks for moisture sources, inspects visible growth and may collect samples for lab analysis. Findings typically guide whether you need full mold removal or only moisture correction. Cost depends on property size and the number of samples; we do not quote prices, and the independent provider gives its own estimate.

Do I need testing if I can see mold?

Not always. Testing is often used when growth is hidden, the source is unclear, or you need documentation. A provider can advise.
